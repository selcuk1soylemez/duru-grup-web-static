## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-trust, prestigious corporate identity celebrating a half-century milestone in national distribution, supply chain management, and FMCG logistics. The visual language blends authoritative heritage with agile, high-throughput digital logistics infrastructure.

- **Brand Tone:** Established authority, nationwide operational reliability, executive prestige, and modern operational precision.
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Corporate Modern fused with Executive Minimalist refinement. It relies on crisp typographic hierarchy, disciplined spatial balance, razor-sharp borders, and curated contrasts of deep navy and vivid scarlet.
- **Audience:** FMCG partners, enterprise suppliers, logistics dispatchers, retail chains, and corporate executive stakeholders requiring rapid data visibility and unquestionable trust.

## Colors
The color architecture honors the corporate identity manual, pairing the primary crimson with the deep anniversary navy.

- **Primary Flag Red (`#D20A11`):** Applied deliberately to key interactive triggers, status alerts, active milestones, and focal callouts. Avoid over-saturating data-dense backgrounds with red; treat it as the precision action layer.
- **50th Anniversary Navy (`#001E61`):** Serves as the primary anchor for headers, side navigation, corporate badges, and data hierarchy structures. It communicates half a century of institutional stability.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:** The base canvas operates on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) layered over soft operational tints (`#F8F9FA` and `#F1F4F9`) to define data tables, card groupings, and vehicle telemetry cards without visual noise.
- **Typography & Neutrals:** Body text uses `#1A1A1A` and `#333333` for high legibility, with muted metadata anchored in cool slate neutrals (`#64748B`).

## Typography
The system unifies the corporate manual's historic identity with operational software performance:

- **Editorial & Milestone Display (`EB Garamond`):** Used for top-tier corporate headers, milestone statements, annual reports, and strategic executive highlights. This mirrors the dignified serif legacy of the core brand wordmark.
- **System Interface & Operations (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean geometry, exceptional numeric clarity, and swift legibility across B2B freight tables, logistics dashboards, inventory lists, and mobile scanner interfaces.
- **Numerics & Waybills:** Numeric identifiers, SKU tags, and cargo metrics utilize tabular numeric figures to ensure precise vertical scanning in enterprise data tables.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column grid system structured on an 8px base rhythm optimizes space for data-dense corporate dashboards and portals.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (`>= 1280px`):** 12 columns, 2rem canvas margin, 1.5rem gutters. Dual-pane view for navigation, operational map tracking, and data tables.
  - **Tablet (`768px - 1279px`):** 8 columns, 1.5rem canvas margin, 1rem gutters. Side navigation collapses into an off-canvas drawer or compact rail.
  - **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4 columns, 1rem canvas margin, 0.75rem gutters. High-priority shipment metrics and action bars pin to the viewport bottom.
- **Rhythm Rules:** Maintain tight 4px–8px relationships between adjacent labels and data inputs. Apply generous 24px–40px structural intervals between isolated module cards to maintain corporate dignity.

## Elevation & Depth
The design relies on architectural layering, clean surface-container tiers, and calibrated low-contrast outlines rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas:** `#F8F9FA` with white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers separated by a precise 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Resting Elevation:** Subdued ambient diffusion (`0 1px 3px rgba(0, 30, 97, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 30, 97, 0.02)`) tinted with corporate navy.
- **Hover & Active Elevation:** Focused lift (`0 8px 24px rgba(0, 30, 97, 0.08)`) with subtle scarlet micro-accents on border highlights.
- **Modals & Flyouts:** Crisp corporate navy backdrop scrim (`rgba(0, 30, 97, 0.45)`) combined with sharp surface isolation.

## Shapes
A disciplined, soft radius style (`0.25rem` / `4px` base) reinforces corporate reliability, structural industrial logistics, and fleet efficiency.

- **Base Elements:** Buttons, text fields, chips, and table containers use `rounded` (4px).
- **Major Surfaces:** Dashboard panels and delivery summary cards employ `rounded-lg` (8px).
- **Asymmetric Signature Motif:** Directorial marketing cards and featured 50th-anniversary hero blocks may selectively adopt the diagonally curved signature silhouette (`top-left: 24px; bottom-right: 24px; top-right: 0px; bottom-left: 0px`), directly echoing the corporate logo usage badge shown in the brand manual.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#D20A11`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px border radius, font `label-md`. Subtle hover transition to `#B0080E`.
- **Secondary Corporate:** Background `#001E61`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover to `#001545`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#001E61` or `#CBD5E1`, text `#001E61`. Hover background in `#F1F4F9`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Transit Status:** Compact pills with a solid 1px border.
  - *Delivered / On Schedule:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `#A7F3D0`.
  - *In Transit / Priority:* Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#001E61`, border `#BFDBFE`.
  - *Delay / Critical Action:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#D20A11`, border `#FECACA`.
- **Anniversary Accent Badge:** `#001E61` solid background with pure white text and gold/crimson micro-accent dot.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard height of 40px for desktop operational efficiency.
- Default surface `#FFFFFF` with 1px outline `#CBD5E1`.
- Focused state applies a 1.5px border in `#001E61` with a faint halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 30, 97, 0.10)`). Error states switch to `#D20A11`.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table headers feature `#F8F9FA` background, uppercase tracking (`label-xs`), and text `#001E61`.
- Row height fixed at 48px with bottom borders in `#E2E8F0`. Hovering a row triggers `#F1F4F9` soft tinting.
- Numeric waybill values and inventory counts use monospace or tabular sans formatting.

### Cargo & Tracking Cards
- White surface, 1px border in `#E2E8F0`, 8px border radius.
- Visual header displays route vectors (Origin → Destination) styled in corporate navy (`#001E61`) with live transit milestones flagged in `#D20A11`.
